## About RFHAS

The Rapid Fielding of High Assurance Software Directorate effort for assuring cyber-physical system is focused on removing the human interpretation from the assurance of CPS through the formalization and automation of the assurance across all pohases of the development process from the early architectural design down to the code and execution infrastructure.
